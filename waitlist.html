What happens from here?

We’d love to be able to say yes to every dog who would enjoy it here, but keeping the group small and familiar is a big part of what makes the Farmhouse work. So, when we’re full, we do keep a waitlist.

Most of our dogs are regulars and become part of a pretty tight-knit little group. Every so often, though, schedules change, families move, or a dog simply doesn’t need daycare anymore, and a space opens up.

When that happens, we look at who has been waiting, the days they need, and which dog may be a good fit for the group. If an opening looks promising for yours, we’ll get in touch and usually arrange a visit outside regular daycare hours.
