A little country. A lot of dog.

The Farmhouse has always been full of life.

It was Jamie’s grandparents’ home, on a property with deep farming roots here in the Valley. These days, the old house has a very different crowd coming through the door.

The story of the place

Long before the dogs moved in.

Jamie’s grandparents lived here, and the Ells family farmed this property for years. A lot of local people still know the family name from farming in the area.

Over the years, that meant cattle, apple orchards, turkey quota, chicken quota, and plenty happening around the property.

Melissa, Jamie, and Weston now have a cow/calf farm here with about 90 cows. The daycare dogs stay on their side of the fence, but the cows are still part of the view. By the time you come up the driveway, there is no mistaking where you are.

Now the old Farmhouse has a new job.

During the day, the house and fenced yard become the dogs’ space. There is room to play, room to settle, and enough time together for everyone’s little habits and preferences to become pretty familiar.

It is a big old house, and it works beautifully for a small group of dogs who want to play hard, find their favourite spot, and make themselves at home.

A home away from home is more than a tagline here.

The Farmhouse is part of the dogs’ actual day. They come through the door, head for the yard, find their friends, claim a spot when it is time to rest, and come back often enough that it all starts to feel pretty familiar.

Meet Melissa

Back to dogs, right here at home.

Melissa worked in dog boarding before spending about ten years working on a dairy farm. Even then, dogs were never exactly missing from her life. She and her family have five of their own now, and have had as many as seven.

Coming back to dog care at the Farmhouse brought a few things together: years of hands-on experience with dogs, life on a working farm, and a setting small enough to actually get to know the dogs who come through the door.

The dogs are supervised throughout the day, and Melissa holds a Canine Health Canada certificate in Canine CPR & Emergency First Aid.
