The Farmhouse

A little country. A lot of dog.

Farmhouse & Fields runs from Jamie’s grandparents’ former home on the family farm in Canning. It’s an old house with plenty of history, a securely fenced yard, and a lot more dogs coming through the door these days.

A family farm

This place has been part of the family for generations.

Jamie’s grandparents lived here, and the Ells family farmed the property for many years. Over time, the farm included cattle, apple orchards, turkeys, and chickens, and the family name is still familiar to a lot of people in the area.

Melissa, Jamie, and Weston are still farming here today, with a cow/calf operation of about 90 cows. The daycare dogs have their own securely fenced space away from the cattle, although they can see them from the yard. You know you’re at a farm before you make it to the front door.

The farmhouse itself is where daycare happens. The dogs have room inside, plenty of time outdoors, and a place they get to know well once they’ve been coming for a while.

These walls have seen a lot over the years. These days, there are a few more nose prints on the windows.

Meet Melissa

Melissa Bailey-O’Hara

Melissa worked in dog boarding before spending about ten years working on a dairy farm. She missed working with dogs, even though dogs themselves were never exactly in short supply. She and her family have five of their own now, and have had as many as seven.

Farmhouse & Fields brought her back to dog care in a way that fits naturally with life here. Keeping the daycare group small means she can be with the dogs throughout the day, get to know them well, and notice the little things that matter.
