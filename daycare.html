Closed-pack daycare

A small group with familiar faces.

Most of the dogs who come to the Farmhouse are regulars, which means they spend their days with dogs they know. New faces do join as spaces open, families change, and needs evolve, but the group stays intentionally small and familiar.

That means Melissa gets to know the dogs, their routines, and how they fit together, instead of managing a different mix every day.

Dogs are supervised throughout the daycare day, both inside and outside.

Hours, rates & flexibility.

Daycare can fit around your week.

Regular clients do not have to commit to the same days every week or book a month at a time. If there is space, bookings can often be made the night before or even the morning of.

A typical day at the Farmhouse.

The exact day changes with the dogs who are here, but the general rhythm stays pretty simple.

Morning

Dogs arrive and there is lots of time to play, move around, and get the day started.

Midday

Dogs who bring lunch are fed separately. Morning half-day dogs head home, afternoon dogs arrive, and the group gets some quieter time.

Afternoon

There is time for another round of play before everyone starts heading home.

Good to know.

A few of the questions that matter once you’re getting into the details.

Meals
Dogs who bring lunch are fed separately at lunchtime.
Vaccinations
Vaccinations are required. The signed Client Agreement contains the current vaccination terms.
Intact dogs
Intact dogs are considered case by case, including age, temperament, and fit with the group.
Payment
Payment can be made by e-transfer, cash, or card through Time To Pet.
Someone else picking up your dog
Someone else can pick up your dog, but Farmhouse & Fields needs to know who will be coming. Payment also needs to be arranged before your dog is released.
Client Agreement
Clients sign the agreement through Time To Pet. Read a copy here.
