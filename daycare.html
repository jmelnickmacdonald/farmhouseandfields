Small numbers. Big days.

Daycare at the Farmhouse.

A small, familiar group, a structured day, and enough flexibility that regular clients can usually book the days they actually need.

A day here

Mornings are usually the busy part.

Dogs arrive between 7:30 and 9:00, everyone says hello, and there is usually a lot of playing before things settle down around lunch.

Some dogs eat at noon. If you bring food and want your dog fed, we have a separate area so lunch can happen individually and without a crowd.

After arrivals, play, and lunch, the pace usually changes. There is nap time, quieter time, and then one more round of play before everyone heads home between 4:00 and 5:00.

The dogs do not all spend the day the same way, and that is part of the point. We know who wants to wrestle, who likes a quieter buddy, and who needs a little space now and then.

A little flexibility goes a long way

You do not have to build your life around daycare.

Once your dog is an established Farmhouse client, booking is meant to be easy.

No weekly commitment

You do not have to commit to a set number of days every week or month. Book the days you need through Time To Pet.

Last-minute can be okay

Some clients do not know until the night before — or even that morning. If we have room, that is okay.

Meals are easy

If your dog needs lunch, bring their food. We feed dogs individually in a separate area.

Vaccines are required

Vaccination requirements apply. We will confirm the current requirements before your dog’s first day.

Intact dogs are case by case

We do consider intact dogs individually, including younger dogs whose vets recommend waiting to spay or neuter.

A small client list on purpose

We cap the client list so established dogs can usually get a spot when they need one.
