Small numbers. Big days.

A good day at the Farmhouse.

Some dogs come in ready to wrestle. Some make a beeline for their favourite friend. Some would happily spend half the day outside, while others know exactly where they want to settle when they need a break. Keeping the group small means we get to know all of that.

A small, familiar group

We keep numbers low and consistent, so the dogs get to know the place, the people, and one another.

Plenty of Farmhouse time

Inside, outside, running, resting, playing, sniffing, or hanging out with a favourite buddy. Not every dog spends the day the same way.

We know our dogs

Their friends, their routines, their funny little habits, and what makes them happiest here. That is a big part of what makes the Farmhouse feel like home.

Home away from home

A little country. A lot of dog.

Farmhouse & Fields really is a farmhouse. The dogs spend their days here with us, with the yard, the fields, and plenty happening around them.

It is comfortable, familiar, a little muddy sometimes, and full of personalities. Exactly the way we like it.
