Real dogs. Real days.

Most of the dogs here are regulars.

They come back week after week, see many of the same friends, and settle into the place pretty quickly. We get to know who likes to wrestle, who prefers a quieter buddy, who wants to be outside, and who will be ready for a nap before lunch.

That familiarity is a big part of what makes the Farmhouse work.
