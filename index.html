Welcome to the Farmhouse.

Your dog’s home away from home.

A dog daycare with room to run, familiar faces, and plenty of time to just be dogs.

Real dogs. Real days.

Most of the dogs here are regulars.

Some come through the door ready to wrestle. Some head straight for the yard. Others have a favourite toy, a favourite nap spot, or a very specific opinion about where they’d like to spend the afternoon.

Over time, we get to know those little routines, and that’s a big part of what makes the days here work.
